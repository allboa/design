What this shows

  • The pole and the antimeridian need no special handling: the mesh lives in source space and every vertex was projected once, upstream.
  • Coastline comes from a line dataset. Land polygons close through the pole, so their rings would draw a false seam along 180 degrees.
  • EPSG:3031 puts the origin at the pole, so float32 offsets stay small here. Other projected CRSs need a coordinateOrigin near the data.
